Product Design

Smart Pocket

Smart Pocket explores a simpler way to manage money: keep one main account, then assign income to named pockets for everyday spending, priorities, savings, and investing. The mobile experience brings balances, budgets, progress, and automatic allocation into one place.

View Figma screens

Problem space

People often manage several goals from the same income. Smart Pocket explores a way to plan that money using one main account and clear pockets for different needs.

Design approach

I designed the key journey from setting monthly income to splitting it into pockets, checking spend and remaining balances, and viewing investments. The screens bring the overall balance and unallocated money forward, then make progress in each pocket easy to scan.

Key design decisions

  • Show total balance and unallocated money near the top, so the starting point is clear.
  • Use named pockets with remaining amounts and progress bars to make budget status quick to scan.
  • Keep spending, savings, and locked pockets filterable in the Pockets screen.
  • Give investing its own space with a portfolio summary and fund details.
  • Make automatic allocation visible rather than hiding it inside settings.

Product screens

This is a design concept. Amounts and returns shown in the screens are sample interface content, not live financial data.

Key features

  • Set monthly income and income timing before assigning money.
  • Organise money into pockets such as rent, emergency, transport, family, giving, fun, and eating out.
  • See pocket amounts and spending progress from the home dashboard.
  • Review spending categories and transactions in one place.
  • Explore an Investments area with a portfolio summary and fund information.
  • See when auto-split is enabled and how income is allocated across planned pockets.

What I would validate next

Next, I would test the pocket model and auto-split setup with people who manage monthly income. I would also validate the language for balances versus budgets, then refine error states, accessibility, and trust cues based on what people find confusing.
